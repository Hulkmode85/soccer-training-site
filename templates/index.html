FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents want to know before signing up.

We offer five age-based group programs: Little Kicks (U6) for ages 4-6, Junior Stars (U8) for ages 7-8, Skill Builders (U10) for ages 9-10, Rising Players (U12) for ages 11-12, and Elite Youth (U14) for ages 13-14. We also offer 1-on-1 Private Training for any player ages 4-16.

Not at all! Our Little Kicks and Junior Stars programs are designed for complete beginners. Every player is placed in the program that matches their age, and our coaches adapt each session to accommodate a range of skill levels within the group. All your child needs is a pair of cleats, shin guards, a water bottle, and a willingness to have fun.

We offer a full refund for cancellations made at least 48 hours before a session. Cancellations within 48 hours receive a credit toward a future session. Monthly package holders can pause or cancel their subscription at any time with 7 days notice before the next billing cycle.

We keep classes intentionally small to maximize individual attention. Little Kicks caps at 8 players, Junior Stars at 10, Skill Builders at 12, and Rising Players and Elite Youth at 14. This ensures every child gets meaningful coaching interaction and plenty of touches on the ball during each session.

Yes! Families enrolling multiple children receive an automatic discount. Little Kicks offers a 15% sibling discount, and all other group programs offer 10% off for each additional sibling. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.

Players should wear comfortable athletic clothing, soccer cleats (turf or firm ground), and shin guards. Bring a filled water bottle and, if the weather calls for it, sunscreen. We provide all training equipment including balls, cones, and bibs. Optional: a positive attitude and a willingness to work hard!

We accept credit and debit cards (processed securely through Stripe), Venmo, and Zelle. You can pay per session or choose our monthly unlimited package for the best value. All payment options are available during the booking process.

Yes! We run week-long intensive summer camps for all age groups from June through August. Camps include daily technical training, tactical sessions, scrimmages, and fun team-building activities.
